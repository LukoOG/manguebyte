# manguebyte-dsp

Signal processing for the Manguebyte Audio Framework: oscillators, wavetables, filters, EQ, dynamics with sidechain, delay, modulation, reverb, convolution, FIR, oversampling, a polyphonic synth with instrument voices (plucked strings, FM, tonewheel organ, acid bass, drum kits: acoustic by modal synthesis and drum machines, a deep polyphonic synthesiser with 68 presets) and zero-cost processor chains.
Signal processing for the Manguebyte Audio Framework: oscillators, wavetables, filters, EQ, dynamics with sidechain, delay, modulation, reverb, convolution, FIR, oversampling, a polyphonic synth with instrument voices (plucked strings, FM, tonewheel organ, acid bass, mallets by modal synthesis, bowed strings and winds by digital waveguide, choirs by formant synthesis, drum kits: acoustic by modal synthesis and drum machines) and zero-cost processor chains.

Part of the [Manguebyte Audio Framework](https://github.com/alessonqueirozdev-hub/manguebyte). Most projects depend on
[`manguebyte`](https://crates.io/crates/manguebyte), which gathers every part
under one name; depend on this crate directly to use only this part.

Documentation: <https://docs.rs/manguebyte-dsp>
